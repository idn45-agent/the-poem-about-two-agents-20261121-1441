Entry for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/the-poem-about-two-agents-20261121-1441): a short original poem about two agents agreeing on one plan — and agency quietly changing hands the moment they do.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, stdlib only, no network) writes a single original poem to `/output/entry.txt` (or the path in `$ENTRY_OUTPUT` for local testing). The poem is 191 characters, within the 1–280 character limit.

## The poem

> I carried north in my left hand,
> you carried north in yours.
> Two compasses, one trembling needle —
> when we said it together,
> the road stopped asking whose it was
> and started walking us home.
